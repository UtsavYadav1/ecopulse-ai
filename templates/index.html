🛡️ Responsible AI

🔍

Transparency

All model metrics (MAE, RMSE, R²) are computed on real held-out data and displayed here. No results are fabricated.

👤

Human Oversight

AI recommendations are advisory only. All energy decisions must be reviewed and validated by qualified humans.

🔒

Privacy

No personally identifiable information is required or collected. The dataset contains only environmental sensor readings.

⚠️

Limitations

This model was trained on a specific low-energy house in Belgium (Jan–May 2016). Performance on other buildings/climates may differ.

🤖

Gemini Explanations

AI explanations are possible interpretations based on supplied data, not verified physical diagnoses of equipment issues.

🌱

SDG Alignment

EcoPulse AI directly supports SDG 7 (Affordable and Clean Energy) and SDG 13 (Climate Action) through energy intelligence.
